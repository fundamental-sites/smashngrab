OUR STORY

SIMPLE FOOD.
SMASHED RIGHT.

At Smash & Grab Burgers, we believe a great burger doesn’t need to be complicated. It just needs good ingredients, big flavor, and a hot griddle

We’re a family-owned business built around doing simple food really well. Our burgers are smashed hard on the griddle to create those crispy, caramelized edges while keeping every bite juicy, cheesy, and packed with flavor. It’s everything we love about a burger in one perfect bite.

And we don’t stop at the burger. Our sauces, dressings, and wing sauces are all made in-house because the little things matter. We put our own spin on the classics so you’re not just getting something you’ve had a hundred times before.

No over-the-top toppings. No unnecessary gimmicks. Just thoughtfully made food with flavors worth coming back for.

We smash it. You grab it.
Simple food. Done right.
